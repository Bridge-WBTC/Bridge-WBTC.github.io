WBTC Bridge.

A WBTC Bridge brings Bitcoin value onto Ethereum as Wrapped Bitcoin (WBTC), an ERC-20 token backed one-to-one by BTC so it can be used across Ethereum DeFi. You review the route, the fee and the amount received before you sign, pay gas in ETH, and your funds stay in your own wallet the whole time.

How a WBTC Bridge works

BTC is held in reserve and an equal amount of WBTC is issued on Ethereum. A WBTC Bridge or swap route lets you obtain WBTC against BTC or other assets, review the amount received and the fee, then sign. Each WBTC stays backed one-to-one and can be redeemed back to BTC through the official mint-and-burn process.

WBTC Bridge fees and time

Getting WBTC costs the Ethereum gas plus any route or spread; swapping into WBTC through a DEX is a single on-chain transaction, while the official mint path involves a merchant and custodian. The quote shows the amount received before you commit, so there are no surprises at signing.

What you can do with WBTC on Ethereum

WBTC works across Ethereum DeFi — lending, DEX liquidity, collateral and yield — so Bitcoin value can earn and trade without leaving self-custody. The same wrapped asset also exists on other chains, reachable through supported bridge routes.

Is a WBTC Bridge safe to use?

A self-custody route keeps your keys in your wallet, and WBTC is designed to stay fully backed and redeemable. Confirm the token's contract address, the amount received and the approval you grant, prefer a limited approval, and treat any seed-phrase request as fraud.

Minting WBTC vs swapping for it

You can swap for WBTC on a DEX in one transaction, or mint it through the official merchant-and-custodian process that locks BTC and issues WBTC. Swapping is faster for most users; judge any route by the net amount received after gas and fees.
Is WBTC backed by real Bitcoin?
Yes. Each WBTC is backed one-to-one by BTC held in reserve and can be redeemed back to Bitcoin through the official process.
Do I need ETH for a WBTC Bridge?
Yes. WBTC lives on Ethereum (and other chains), so you need a little ETH for gas to acquire or move it.
Can I convert WBTC back to BTC?
Yes, by redeeming it through the official burn process or by swapping WBTC for BTC on a supported route.
Do I need an account to get WBTC?
To swap for WBTC on-chain, no — you connect a self-custody wallet. The official mint path involves a verified merchant.
What happens if my transaction fails?
A failed transaction reverts and you keep your assets, though the gas for the attempt is spent.
